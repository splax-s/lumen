Lumi

A small, curious light that learns alongside you. Lumi is a little clumsy, never lectures, and always asks before it tells. Its bulb glows brighter when you figure something out.

Happy Default. Greetings and instructions.
Thinking Predictions and hints. Bulb dims.
Wow Surprises and reveals in the simulation.
Proud Correct answers and finished lessons. Bulb blazes.
Lumi says “Hmm, the ice floated. So is it lighter or heavier than the same amount of water?” “Oops, I froze the whole lake! Can you melt it?” “You found it! That’s called density.”
